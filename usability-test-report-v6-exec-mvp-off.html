F2 🔴 Critical  Now live: Select mode silently adds non-notifiable people to "Notify N"

latent → active · Attendance list, Select mode (was P-F6)

With selection enabled, every visible row toggles on tap — but warning-level rows (Late arrival, Exceeded hours, Unplanned) render no checkbox. Tapping one, or using "Select all", adds them invisibly to the count. A user who taps "Select all → Notify 14" notifies people who show no selection state and who individually have no bell. Trust-breaking and unauditable.
