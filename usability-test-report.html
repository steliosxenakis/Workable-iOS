Verdict

V5 is the most scannable version as shipped — 12/12 detection for all three personas with zero false alarms; V6 (current default) matches its speed but flattens severity into all-red, costing 3 false alarms per scan. 6 issues · 2 critical — apply S1 (one line) and V6 becomes the recommended default.
