F4 🟡 Major  Stat cards are secret filters — identical to Today's static chips

Max · Attendance list stats row

Max tapped one expecting a drill-down, never realised a filter was on, and walked away believing only 1 of his 2 line issues existed. A filter that hides people without clearly saying so produces wrong decisions, not just friction.

Verdict

V6 Exec identifies issues well for a systematic user, but the default build promises action it cannot deliver — 0 of 3 personas could notify anyone, and one walked away with wrong data about his own team. Fix the notify path (F1) and the count reconciliation (F2) before any exec-facing demo.
