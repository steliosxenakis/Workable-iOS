v8 + FAB mechanics What's different in this configuration
Three behaviour changes vs the base run: (1) Per-row bell icons are hidden (hidesBellForFab = true). (2) The "Select" section-header button is hidden (!usesFabNotifyStyle gate). (3) A 56 px teal FAB bell button is permanently visible at the bottom-right of the Attendance tab.

Tapping the FAB calls enterFabSelection(): it saves the current filters, force-sets selectedFilters = actionableFilterCategories (danger-level only), sets isSelecting = true, and pre-selects all currently visible danger-level employees via selectedForNotification = selectableEmployeeIDs. The floating bar then shows Cancel / Deselect all / Notify all (N).

Swipe-to-notify is unchanged and still works for danger-level rows when NOT in selection mode.

N1 Major — new in v8+FAB FAB pre-selects ALL danger employees across the whole list, not just direct reports
LilyPriya enterFabSelection() selectableEmployeeIDs
enterFabSelection() sets selectedForNotification = selectableEmployeeIDs, which is every danger-level employee in the filtered list — not just the user's direct reports. In a real org with 20+ employees on the Attendance tab, tapping the FAB pre-selects all of them. An HR Admin managing a small team could accidentally bulk-notify dozens of employees they don't manage. There's no scope confirmation before tapping "Notify all (N)".
